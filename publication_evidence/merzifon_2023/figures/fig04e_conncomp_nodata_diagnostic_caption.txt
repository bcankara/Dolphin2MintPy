Fig. 4e Connected-component matrix-equivalence diagnostic illustrating the effect of nodata encoding on integer-valued raster comparison. (a) Peak connectComponent metrics (max |diff|, mean |diff|, RMSE) across the 84 date pairs under two protocols: a raw integer comparison (red bars) that interprets the Dolphin GeoTIFF nodata value as a regular sample, and a nodata-aware comparison (green bars) that masks the source nodata before the comparison. The raw protocol records peak max |diff| = 3.28e+04, peak mean |diff| = 234, and peak RMSE = 2.77e+03; the nodata-aware protocol records 0.0 for every metric. (b) Distribution of the per-pair exact-match ratio for connectComponent under the same two protocols; under the raw comparison the per-pair exact-match ratio cluster is 0.9929, while the nodata-aware comparison yields exactly 1.0000 for all 84 pairs. The raw apparent discrepancy is fully explained by the integer-encoding asymmetry between the Dolphin source raster (uint16, nodata = 65535) and the MintPy HDF5 target dataset (int16, nodata = -32768).
